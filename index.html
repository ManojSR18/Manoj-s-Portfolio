Premier League 2026-27 Predictions
Predicts every remaining Premier League match with a machine-learning model: a blend of three Poisson goals models trained on 829 matches and 25,000+ player-match rows (xG, xA, ratings, tackles). It plays out the rest of the season with realistic scores, predicts scorers, assists and player ratings, and uses an analysis of variance to separate skill from luck. Runs entirely in the browser.
