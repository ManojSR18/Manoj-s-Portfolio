## 🚀 Projects



### [Premier League 2026-27 Predictions](projects/premier-league/)
**What it does:** Predicts every remaining Premier League match and plays out the rest of the season with realistic scores, predicted scorers, assists and player ratings. A second page gives matchweek predictions, title and relegation odds and club pages.
**Tech used:** `Python` `pandas` `scikit-learn` `statsmodels` `JavaScript`
**Highlights:** A blend of three Poisson goals models trained on 829 matches and 25,000+ player-match rows. It was tested matchweek by matchweek (about 48% of results called correctly, with well-calibrated probabilities). An analysis of variance shows about 84% of a result is luck.
**Links:** [Predicted Season](projects/premier-league/) · [Match predictions](projects/premier-league/fulltime-forecast.html) · [Repo](https://github.com/ManojSR18/champions-league)

### [Traffic Signal Optimization](projects/traffic-signal/)
**What it does:** Forecasts NYC traffic volume 15 minutes ahead and uses the forecast to retime a traffic signal.
**Tech used:** `Python` `scikit-learn` `JavaScript`
**Highlights:** Compares a linear model, a decision tree and a random forest on a chronological train/test split, then optimises the green-time split to cut queues and delay.
**Links:** [Live demo](projects/traffic-signal/) · [Repo](https://github.com/ManojSR18/Traffic-Signal-Project)
