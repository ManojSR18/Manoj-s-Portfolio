Building the featuressteps 6–13

Each record looks one hour into the past and 15 minutes into the future. A row only survives if all five neighbouring counts are exactly 15 minutes apart, which is what the timestamp check enforces.

X.head() after get_dummies

Train and test splitstep 18

The split is chronological, not random: the models learn from 2025 and are judged on 2026 they have never seen. A random split would leak the future into training through the lag columns.

Training three modelssteps 19–25

Same features, three ways of using them: a straight line, one deep tree, then a hundred trees voting. Metrics are computed on the 2026 test set only.

Error on the test set

Lower is better. MAE is the average miss in vehicles per 15 minutes; RMSE punishes big misses.

Retiming the signalsteps 26–29

The forecast becomes demand: northbound plus southbound is the north–south phase, eastbound plus westbound is east–west. Sixty seconds of green get divided between them to minimise the delay proxy NS·((C−gns)/C)² + EW·(gns/C)².
